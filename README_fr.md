## 🍌 Introduction

Bienvenue dans le dépôt de prompts MAI-Image-2.5 ! 🤗

**Nous rassemblons des prompts et des exemples d'images de haute qualité pour MAI-Image-2.5 et MAI-Image-2.5-Flash de Microsoft, couvrant portraits, illustration, affiches et retouche d'image.**

MAI-Image-2.5 est le premier modèle d'image entièrement maison de Microsoft, présenté à la Microsoft Build 2026 : entraîné de zéro sans distillation, il se classe #2 sur l'Image Edit Arena et #3 en text-to-image. Il est conçu pour un éclairage photoréaliste, des retouches précises préservant l'identité et le texte, et un excellent rapport qualité-prix.

<a href="https://x.com/arena/status/2061887242579382660"><img src="images/leaderboard/output.jpg" width="560" alt="Image Edit Arena leaderboard: MAI-Image-2.5 ranks #2 with a score of 1401"></a>

***MAI-Image-2.5 se classe #2 sur l'Image Edit Arena (score 1401), devant toutes les variantes de Nano Banana.*** (classement par [@arena](https://x.com/arena))

</div>

La plupart des cas de ce dépôt proviennent de X/Twitter, de communautés de créateurs et de démos publiques. L'auteur d'origine est crédité pour chaque cas.

Essayez-le sur Evolink: [MAI-Image-2.5](https://evolink.ai/models)

Si cela vous est utile, pensez à mettre une étoile. ⭐

> [!NOTE]
> Ce dépôt se concentre sur des schémas de prompts réutilisables et des cas de référence. Les prompts marqués **reconstruit** sont des reconstitutions fidèles d'un résultat présenté dont l'auteur n'a pas publié le texte exact — ils sont clairement signalés pour que vous ne les confondiez jamais avec les mots exacts de l'auteur.

### Case 7: [Flooded Taiga Night Train Edit](https://x.com/SqueakAlGaib/status/2063043138034491659) (by [@SqueakAlGaib](https://x.com/SqueakAlGaib))

| Before | After |
| :----: | :---: |
| <img src="images/comparison_case7/output0.jpg" width="300" alt="Original aerial night shot of a red train moving through the snowy Siberian taiga"> | <img src="images/comparison_case7/output1.jpg" width="300" alt="Edited flooded taiga night train scene generated by MAI-Image-2.5"> |

**Input:** Upload the original aerial train image before editing.

**Prompt:**

```
Edit the attached image of the aerial night shot of the red Trans-Siberian locomotive moving through the snowy Siberian taiga forest.

Transform the landscape between all the trees into a vast, shallow floodwater surface. Every gap between trees — the ground, the snow-covered forest floor, all open spaces — is now covered in still, dark, mirror-flat water approximately 1–1.5 meters deep. The pine trees now stand in this water up to their first or second branches, their trunks disappearing into the black reflective surface below.

The water must be photorealistic and physically accurate. It is still and glassy — not turbulent — so it acts as a near-perfect dark mirror. It must reflect the following elements accurately and in correct perspective: the deep dark-blue night sky above, the white steam/smoke plume from the locomotive, the warm amber glow from the train's passenger car windows, and the cold white headlight beam from the engine. Each reflection should have a slight wavering distortion consistent with very shallow still water, not completely flat glass.

The train tracks must appear to run just at or slightly above the waterline — as if elevated on a low embankment that barely clears the flood level. The train itself is completely unchanged — same red livery, same position, same heading, same steam plume, same lights. Do not alter any aspect of the locomotive or its cars.

The snow on the branches of the trees remains — the flooding came from below, not precipitation. Upper branches are still snow-laden; the lower submerged trunks show waterlogged bark texture where they meet the flood surface.

The overall color palette shifts slightly — the ground, previously white snow, is now reflective black water, making the red train and amber windows pop even more dramatically as the only warm elements in a cold, flooded, nocturnal landscape. Maintain the aerial 45-degree drone perspective exactly. Do not alter the sky, the steam, the forest canopy silhouette, or the train in any way.
```

> [!NOTE]
> The tweet is a community comparison post, but the MAI-Image-2.5 edit prompt is fully public and reusable. This case is kept mainly as an image-editing reference for large-scale environment transformation while preserving subject identity and camera angle.
